Three approaches for the first-time user experience — same content, very different feel

A — Cinematic full-bleed

Real city photography fills the screen. Each slide has its own destination backdrop. Text overlays ride on top with a dark gradient. Feels like a travel magazine. Each swipe cross-fades photo + text with subtle parallax.

travel_explore

From Tokyo backstreets to Lisbon hilltops

B — Illustrated + motion

No photos — each slide has its own abstract animated scene built from CSS gradients, glows, and motion graphics. The persona slide might show a silhouette assembling. The map slide shows animated pins. Feels premium and app-native.

Pin hidden gems on an interactive map

C — App screenshot reveal

Real app screenshots inside a phone frame, with glowing callout annotations pointing at key features. Each slide zooms into or animates a specific part of the UI. Shows the actual product — sets honest expectations, builds anticipation for features they're about to use.
