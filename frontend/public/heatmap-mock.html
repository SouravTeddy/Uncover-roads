Heatmap zoom behaviour

Proposed: heatmap dominant when zoomed out → fade as pins appear when zooming in

City overview
zoom ~8–10
Heatmap at full opacity. Broad blobs show where interesting areas cluster. Pins present but tiny — heatmap dominates.
District view
zoom ~11–13
Crossfade zone. Heatmap softening, pins becoming readable. User is zooming into a blob they found.
Street level
