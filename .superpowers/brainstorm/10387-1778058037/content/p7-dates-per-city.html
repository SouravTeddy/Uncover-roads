A — Toast CTA → inline date picker per city

When a new city is detected, the toast has an "Add dates" CTA. Tapping it expands date pills for that city only — right there on the map, no navigation. Dates remain optional (dismiss toast to skip).

B — Dates set from Journey screen city cards

No date prompt on the map. Each city card on the Journey screen has a "+ Add dates" tap target. Dates are set from there. Journey screen is already the planning hub — keep all date editing there.
