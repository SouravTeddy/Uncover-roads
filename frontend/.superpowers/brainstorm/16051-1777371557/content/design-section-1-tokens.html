How theme toggle works

Theme stored in localStorage('ur_theme') and in Zustand store.
On mount, app reads preference and sets document.documentElement.dataset.theme = 'light' | 'dark'.
Tailwind @theme holds dark defaults. A [data-theme=light] CSS block overrides every token.
One attribute flip → entire app switches instantly, no re-render needed.
