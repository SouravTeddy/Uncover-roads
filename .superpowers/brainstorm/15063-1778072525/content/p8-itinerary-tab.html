Behaviour notes

Saved filter on map — when user hearts a pin, a "Saved" filter chip appears in the map filter bar briefly, then stays. Tapping it filters map to show only hearted pins. The heart state persists and merges into this screen.

Masonry layout — first place card in each city group is tall (2 rows). Rest are compact. Creates visual rhythm without feeling like a list. No photo API needed — emoji placeholder + gradient background per category works until photos load.

Events saved from map — when user saves a local event (long-press or heart on event card), it appears in the relevant city group here with amber styling. Distinct from places.

"Open on map" tap — loads map centred on that city with travel dates pre-filled. If dates are in the past: sky-blue nudge asks "When are you going? We'll check events, weather and opening days." — honest about why we need the date.

Travel dates copy everywhere — whenever we ask for dates, the reason is always visible: "We use this to check events, weather and opening days." Never just "set your travel dates" with no explanation.
