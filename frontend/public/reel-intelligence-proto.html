Conflict resolution — priority hierarchy
How we handle conflicts
priority_high
Weather always wins. Rain or extreme heat overrides walk/scenic suggestions. The transit row in the logistics bar strikes through the walking option and shows the alternative mode. The narrative says why in plain English.
swap_vert
Crowd peak + user pick = stay + warn, don't remove. If a user added a stop that hits crowd peak at their visit time, we don't swap it — we show the crowd badge as "Busy period" and the narrative explains we moved it to the least-bad window.
directions_walk
Scenic card suppressed when rain, shown when clear. If a scenic walk card would appear between two stops but it's raining at that time, the card is not emitted. Instead, the transit row on the next stop card shows the alternative.
check_circle
We always acknowledge the conflict. Never silently override. The user should know a decision was made on their behalf and why — even one sentence is enough.
